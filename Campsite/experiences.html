Four ways to spend a day here

Experiences

Everything below runs from camp — no need to drive into the park for most of it. Activities marked Park Fee require Kenya Wildlife Service park entry; everything else happens on the boundary land we manage ourselves.

Camping

Three ways to sleep here, all inside our electric perimeter fence along the river.

  • Bring Your Own Tent — level, shaded pitches with access to shared ablutions and a communal fire pit.
  • Furnished Walk-In Tents — canvas tents with real beds, solar lighting, and a private veranda facing the park.
  • Riverside Family Site — a larger pitch for two tents, closest to the dam, best for groups with children.

Firewood, drinking water, and a supervised campfire are included at every site after dark — the fence keeps wildlife out, not you in.

Wildlife Viewing

From the camp deck (no park fee)

Our raised deck looks directly into the park's open plains. Most mornings and evenings you'll see zebra, giraffe, warthog, and various antelope without leaving camp. Binoculars are provided at check-in.

Guided game drive Park Fee

A half-day drive into the park itself with our resident guide, in search of lion, rhino, and the park's resident buffalo herds. Park entry fees are billed separately at KWS rates.

Guided Nature Walks

Two armed rangers lead every walk along the buffer land outside the park fence — close enough to read fresh tracks, far enough to stay safely clear of the larger grazers.

  1. Sunrise Bird Walk (1.5 hrs) — riverine forest along the Mbagathi, best for the park's 400+ recorded bird species.
  2. Track & Sign Walk (2 hrs) — learning to read footprints, dung, and browse lines with a ranger.
  3. Sundowner Walk (2 hrs) — ends at a ridge overlooking both the park and the city skyline for sunset.
Is it safe to walk outside the fence?

All walks are led by two KWS-licensed armed rangers who know this stretch of boundary well. Routes are chosen daily based on where wildlife has been moving, and walks are paused or rerouted if animals are too close for a safe distance.

Guided Fishing

Catch-and-release fishing on our stretch of the Mbagathi River and the camp's dam, for tilapia and barbel. Rods, bait, and a guide are included — you keep nothing but the story.
